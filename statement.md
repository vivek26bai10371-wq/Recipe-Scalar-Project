# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
When cooking for a different number of people than a recipe is written for, it is difficult to manually recalculate every ingredient quantity. It is also hard to know how much money to spend on groceries.

## Objective
To build a program that automatically scales recipe ingredients according to the required servings and generates a grocery list with an estimated budget.

## Solution
The program stores prices of common ingredients, lets the user enter a recipe, calculates the scaling factor (required servings ÷ original servings), multiplies every ingredient quantity by it, and adds up the cost. Ingredients from all scaled recipes are combined into a single grocery list with a total budget.

## Expected Outcome
A menu-based tool that saves time, reduces food waste, and helps plan grocery spending.
